# Changelog

## 0.9.0
- **Achievements, grand opening:** the first time you open the page each session, one star pulls the whole sky into itself and bursts; eight comets fly out and draw the constellations one by one, the title is struck over the galaxy and the land rises into view. It has its own score. Click to skip; later openings use the short loader.
- **Achievements, the lookout:** the stargazer and the cat now have a place of their own: a wooden deck under a string of lights, with a tent, a campfire, a lake, an observatory sweeping the sky and a rocket on its pad. Constellation names are always on the map.
- **Mythological dig:** the camera now films the dig up close, swinging round you and kicking with every strike; the ground cracks with light, and a pillar of it breaks out when the creature or the chest comes up.
- **Sounds:** every catalog's animation has its own set. Mob Drops keep VOYAGER; Mythological creatures get RELIC (lyre, drums, bronze, blade) and treasure gets TROVE (spade, hinges, coins, music box), all timed to the dig.
- Mythological animations: you dig, the right creature climbs out and is cut down (or a chest rises and opens), all on a little planet in space. Twelve creatures, treasure, No Drops, RNG.
- Drop text has a new format: a small headline, the name, a hairline, the value, and one line with chance, odds and Magic Find.
- Achievements: a new loading screen (a wheel of eight lights) and a Star Atlas list of every constellation.
- Menu: icons without backgrounds, a trail instead of the sidebar tree, cleaner Catalog pages, Mob Drops on one page, drop chances back under each drop.
- Visuals page is much lighter (its background aurora was thousands of tiny pieces; the sky preview redraws less often; the music folder is read on a side thread).
- Flicker guard: Stardrops watches for a flickering sky by itself. If one of its parts causes it, that part switches off and chat tells you which; if something else does, chat says so.
- Drops now play RNG by default only when worth your RNG price (new in Settings > Prices, 10m to start); cheaper drops play Normal. Your own picks stay.
- New sound pack for every Mob Drop: VOYAGER, sonar pings, thrusters, warp whooshes, crystal bells and a starry choir.
- The Drop Library is now the Catalog. Visuals opens each room's settings under its three big buttons. Each main page has its own sky, and Stardrops has a new mark.
- Mob Drops: browse by Slayers or by Location, with new animated icons for every place and each mob's own head.
- Every location has its own world in the drop animation (Deep Caverns, Catacombs, Moonglade Marsh, Torrhus Canyon, Spooky Festival, Crystal Hollows and more), and every mob its own creature: skeletons, zombies, knights, slimes, witches, golems, the Shiny Pig, Galatea's crawlers, Nessie and the Loch Emperor, bats, creepers.
- Mob Drops: every mob's rare drops, sorted by where it spawns (Hub, Spider's Den, The End, Crimson Isle, the Mines, the Park, Galatea, Spooky Festival, Catacombs) and by mob, with their chances. Rare drops play RNG by default.
- Spotify shows as a small pop-up box at the top right of the menu.
- The big centred page cards are back; the Quick switches are gone.
- New menu look: a rounded window with an Opacity setting, a tidier sidebar in groups (Browse, Customize), and Animations, Sounds and Chat together as Drop effects.
- Drop Library pages: a clear header with counts, one bar to change every drop, and rows that show what plays and whether you brag in chat. Point at Normal to make it RNG; RNG drops shine gold.
- Quick actions: click the search box or press Ctrl F to test a drop, switch things on or off, jump to a page or find any drop.
- Icons look centred, not just measured centred.
- Night sky: constellations and planets stay above the horizon all night.
- Mob Drops show the right creature for every drop (checked against the drop tables), and a new Diamond Goblin.
- Smooth animations with Gnetum installed.
- The menu: compact page headers and Quick switches on every tab; real live previews (your Now Playing card, the real night sky); HUDs has no preview.
- Cosmos: Try a meteor / shower is lighter and brings you back to the menu. New Deep sky (nebulae, a galaxy) and Comet; the aurora stays low over the horizon.
- Lyrics: Drift removed; clearer style buttons; long text scrolls calmly.
- Visuals menu remade: three rooms (Music, Cosmos, HUDs) with live previews, quick switches, and one feature open at a time.
- Now Playing: the rocket no longer covers the artist, several artists read cleanly, no bottom bar. New Peek option.
- Drop moments: a new drop never restarts a meteor shower that is still burning.
- Smoother, lighter animations; the night sky no longer shimmers or steps.
- "First day" achievements are now daily runs of 24 hours.
- Night sky: Milky Way and Planets switches. HUDs can stay on screen during drop animations.
- Server: no more daily storage limit errors once moved to D1; achievements no longer stay "waiting for the server".
- Mob Drops show the creature that drops the item: Bladesoul, Barbarian Duke, Ashfang, Mage Outlaw, Ghost, Ice Walker, Bal and the Zealot join the Magma Boss, the Dragon and the Automaton.
- Cosmos: "Only night" keeps the sky steady whatever other mods do to the time; constellations stand out more.
- HUD pieces are named for what they show (Y level, Speed, Compass...). The speed gauge is steady while you run.
- Now Playing: a record peeks out behind the cover, a shine crosses it, the destination pulses with the beat.
- Fixed: the Spotify button covering the left menu while searching.
- Mob Drops animate: the Crimson Isle (the Magma Boss), the End (the Ender Dragon) and the Mines (an Automaton) each have their own creature and world.
- Slayer: real burning meteorites that crater the ground, and a far bigger RNG: six star-fires, a held breath, a golden star that falls like a hammer.
- A new, cinematic sound set for Mob Drops: war drums, horns, choir.
- Cosmos: the night sky is now a real sky in your world with your Achievement constellations in it; a meteor burns across the screen when a drop lands, a meteor shower for RNG.
- Visuals > Instruments: eight HUD instruments (compass, altimeter, speed, coordinates, ping and FPS, real-time clock, session log, and the celestial clock).
- Now Playing is a little smaller and fuller; you can play your own MP4 / M4A music files (Visuals > Music > Your files).
- Fixed: animation previews in the menu played in the middle of the screen.
- Slayer scene: the moon and planets are different every time (side, size, colours, rings, bands); Tarantula is a proper hairy tarantula that drops in on its thread, in a den of webbed dead trees, egg sacs and bones.
- Slayer scene: a solid, pixel-textured floor in each boss's own blocks that darkens into fog and a vignette (no more see-through tiles), and a far more detailed backdrop: dusk sky, pixel moon, a ringed planet, layered hills and silhouettes with haze, bats and rain.
- **Stardrops:** FancyDrops is now Stardrops, space themed from top to bottom. Your settings and saved data move over on first launch.
- **Slayer, the Stardrops way:** the boss rises out of its spawn on the curve of a giant planet, its glowing horizon and a moon behind it, and roars at you. Falling stars strike it on every beat; the last one shatters it into stardust that gathers into your drop, and the strikes join into its constellation. RNG stars climb the rarity colours and end in gold.
- **Slayer sounds:** five moments per animation (rise, roar, the falling stars, the reveal, after), three sounds to choose from for each. Picks from the old set move to the same take of the new one.
- **Text duration:** set how long the drop text stays (Animations > Text Changes > Layout).
- **Other categories:** their new animations are in the works. Drops there send their chat alert, and the Drop Library shows a WIP panel.
- **Menu:** space glass tiles, a Spotify Linked chip, new space icons, `/sd` and `/stardrops` commands, and `/sd edit` to move every HUD.
- **Music:** Now Playing voyages between song worlds, there's a hyperspace song intro, and Space Ambience while music plays.
- **Achievements:** real constellations, a stargazer and their cat on the hill, and a sky with depth.
- **Lyrics:** words now sit at slightly different heights and pop in. Now Playing and the song intro move more smoothly.
- **Fixes:** music keys you bound before the rename keep their keys. `/sd flicker` steps through the mod's parts to find what makes the sky flicker.
